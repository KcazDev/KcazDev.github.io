HUNTER SYSTEM

Fitness, reimagined as a progression-based RPG.

Hunter System is a mobile fitness prototype that rewards real-world training with XP, levels, ranks and unlocks. Daily quests, workout-based Gates and party challenges bring game progression into a personal fitness experience.

WHAT I BUILT

Where fitness meets systems design

Progression

Ranks, earned titles, level-ups, XP and character stats give fitness milestones game-like feedback.

Training workflows

Exercise logging, daily quest checklists and Gates map activity to progression rewards.

Social motivation

A two-person Party view shows XP, streaks and competition through weekly challenges.

Personal tracking

Vessel entries support weight and body measurements, and an Items interface displays equipped rewards.
