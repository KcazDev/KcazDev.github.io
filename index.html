Multilingual RAG System

Developed during the 2026 NSF Virtual REU at Portland State University. I built and evaluated a Multi lingual news question answering pipeline using BGE-M3 retrieval, evidence deduplication, MMR diversification, and LLM answer generation for Spanish and Arabic questions.

Research results: Reduced retrieved evidence from 20 to 12 sentences (40% less context), with comparable answer quality: 75/100 versus 73/100 in manual scoring across 50 questions. Raw Top 20 retrieval recovered both supporting articles in 40/50 questions; filtered Top 12 recovered both in 37/50.

Applied research in multilingual AI, information retrieval, and reliable question answering.

During the 2026 NSF Virtual Research Experiences for Undergraduates program, Computational Modeling Serving Portland, I developed and evaluated a multilingual RAG system under the mentorship of Dr. Ameeta Agrawal.

2026 · RESEARCH MANUSCRIPT · NSF VIRTUAL REU18-PAGE MANUSCRIPT

Building a Multilingual Retrieval Augmented Question Answering System Using XRAG

Zackery Lake · Computational Modeling Serving Portland · Portland State University

This research compares BM25, multilingual E5, hybrid BM25–E5 retrieval, and sentence level BGE-M3 for Multi lingual question answering system over news articles. On 50 XRAG questions in Arabic and Spanish, BGE-M3 Top 20 retrieved sentences representing both supporting articles for 40 questions, with an average support article recall of 0.88. A preliminary 10 question generation evaluation scored 18/20.
